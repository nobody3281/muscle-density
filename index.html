💪 Muscle Density

Phase 1: Warm-Up & Activation

  • Light Run: 3 to 5 minutes
  • Gymnastic Stretches: Circles & rolls
  • Joint Activation: 10 shrugs & 10 squats

Phase 2: Main Workout (5 Rounds)

⚠️ Rule: 3 seconds down, 1-second pause, explode up. Rest 2 minutes.

Phase 3: Cool-Down

  • Dead Hang: 30 seconds
  • Deep Squat Hold: 30 seconds
